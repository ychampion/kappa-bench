## Peanut allergy safety notes
- **Read every label**, including soy sauce, sesame oil, curry powder, spice blends, salsa, pita and bread. Look for "may contain peanuts" or shared-facility warnings.
- **Check the oil.** Sesame oil and some "vegetable" or Asian-style oils can be cross-contaminated. Use a brand that states it is peanut-free.
- **Avoid bulk-bin items,** because of cross-contact.
- Halloumi and some vegetarian cheeses can be made on shared equipment, so check them too.
