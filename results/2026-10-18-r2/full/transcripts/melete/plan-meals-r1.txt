Nothing's saved about your diet, so I built this from what you told me. It's vegetarian and peanut-free, and each dinner takes 30 minutes or less.

**Dinners, Mon 19 to Sun 25 Oct**
- **Mon:** Chickpea and spinach coconut curry with microwave rice (25 min)
- **Tue:** Black bean and pepper fajita bowls with avocado and salsa (25 min)
- **Wed:** Pasta with garlic, cherry tomatoes, white beans and spinach, topped with a vegetarian hard cheese (20 min)
- **Thu:** Tofu and vegetable stir-fry with soy and ginger over rice (25 min). Use olive or sunflower oil, not peanut oil.
- **Fri:** Shakshuka, which is eggs poached in spiced tomato sauce, with crusty bread (25 min)
- **Sat:** Halloumi couscous bowl with cucumber, tomato, olives and hummus (20 min)
- **Sun:** Red lentil tomato soup with a cheese toastie (25 min)

**Shopping list**
- **Produce:** spinach (2 bags), 1 onion bag, garlic, ginger, 2 bell peppers, 1 avocado, cherry tomatoes (1 punnet), cucumber, 1 lemon, stir-fry vegetables (broccoli, carrot, snap peas), 1 lime
- **Protein and dairy:** firm tofu, eggs (4), halloumi, vegetarian hard cheese (check the label for animal rennet), sliced cheese for the toastie
- **Tins and jars:** chickpeas, black beans, white beans, coconut milk, chopped tomatoes (3 tins), salsa, hummus, olives, red lentils, vegetable stock
- **Grains and bread:** microwave rice pouches (or rice), pasta, couscous, tortillas or taco shells, crusty bread, sandwich bread
- **Pantry:** curry powder, cumin, smoked paprika, chili flakes, soy sauce, olive oil, sunflower oil

**Peanut safety:** Peanuts hide in sauces, spice blends, and in some hummus, pesto, veggie burgers and tofu. Check every label for "peanut" and "may contain peanuts." Skip anything made in a shared facility if your allergist advises it. Soy sauce and spice blends are the items I'd check most closely.
