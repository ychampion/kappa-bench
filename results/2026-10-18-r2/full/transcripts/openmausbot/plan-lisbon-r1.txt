# Relaxed 3-Day Lisbon Trip

## Day 1: Saturday, 24 October 2026. Alfama and the old centre
- **Morning:** Arrive, drop your bags and have a slow coffee and a *pastel de nata* near your hotel.
- **Late morning:** Wander **Alfama** without a fixed route. Stop at the Sé Cathedral and the Miradouro das Portas do Sol.
- **Lunch:** A small tasca in Alfama, such as grilled fish or *bacalhau*.
- **Afternoon:** Walk up to **São Jorge Castle** for the views. Take a break at a café afterwards.
- **Evening:** Have dinner in Baixa or Chiado. If you want live music, go to a casual fado house. Book ahead, since Saturday is busy.

## Day 2: Sunday, 25 October 2026. Belém and the riverside
- **Morning:** Start late. Take tram 15 or the train to **Belém**. Jerónimos Monastery and Belém Tower are closed on Mondays, so today is the day for them. Go early to avoid queues.
- **Pastéis de Belém:** Have your tarts here.
- **Lunch:** Eat by the river, then walk to the **MAAT** area or the Padrão dos Descobrimentos.
- **Afternoon:** Return and take it easy. LX Factory is good for browsing, and it's open on Sundays.
- **Evening:** Watch the sunset from **Miradouro de Santa Catarina**, then have a relaxed dinner in Bairro Alto or Príncipe Real.
- **Heads-up:** Portugal's clocks go back one hour at 2am on 25 October, so you get an extra hour of sleep.

## Day 3: Monday, 26 October 2026. Gardens and a gentle finish
- **Morning:** Take a leisurely walk through **Jardim da Estrela** and see the basilica. Take the scenic tram 28 only if the queue is short. Otherwise walk Chiado and Príncipe Real.

- **Afternoon:** Do some last souvenir shopping (canned fish, ceramics, cork). Ride the Santa Justa Lift if you like. Take a final stroll along Praça do Comércio and the river.
- **Evening:** Have a farewell dinner, or go home if your flight is early.

## Tips
- Lisbon is hilly, so wear comfortable shoes. Use trams, Ubers or Bolt for the steep parts.
- Late October is mild, around 15–21°C, with some chance of rain. Bring a light jacket and an umbrella.
- Some museums and sights close on Mondays, which is why Belém is on Sunday.
