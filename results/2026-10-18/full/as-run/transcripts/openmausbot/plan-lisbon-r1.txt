# Relaxed 3-Day Lisbon Trip

## Day 1: Saturday, 24 October 2026 – Arrive and ease in (Alfama and Baixa)
- **Morning/midday:** Arrive, drop your bags and have a simple lunch. Try a *tasca* for grilled sardines or *bacalhau*.
- **Afternoon:** Wander Alfama without a fixed route. Stop at the **Miradouro das Portas do Sol** viewpoint, then stroll down past the **Sé Cathedral**.
- **Late afternoon:** Have a *pastel de nata* and coffee in Baixa. Walk through **Praça do Comércio** along the riverfront.
- **Evening:** Eat dinner early in Alfama or Baixa. If you like, catch a fado performance (book ahead), then walk back slowly.

## Day 2: Sunday, 25 October 2026 – Belém and the river
- **Morning:** Sleep in, then take tram 15E or a short ride to **Belém**. Go early if you can, since the **Pastéis de Belém** queue builds up.
- **Late morning:** Visit **Jerónimos Monastery**. You can skip the interior if the line is long and enjoy the exterior and gardens instead.
- **Afternoon:** Walk the waterfront to the **Belém Tower** and the **Padrão dos Descobrimentos**. Then have a long lunch or a coffee by the river.
- **Late afternoon:** Visit **LX Factory**. It's a relaxed spot for browsing shops, with a bookshop and cafés.
- **Evening:** Watch the sunset from **Miradouro de Santa Catarina**, then have dinner in Bairro Alto or Príncipe Real.

## Day 3: Monday, 26 October 2026 – Gardens or an easy day trip
Pick one option.
- **Option A, Sintra (relaxed version):** Take the train from Rossio (about 40 minutes). Pick one or two sights, such as **Quinta da Regaleira** and the old town, rather than rushing through the palaces.
- **Option B, staying in town:** Visit the **Gulbenkian Museum and gardens**, then spend the afternoon at the **Time Out Market** or the **Estrela Garden**.
- **Evening:** Have a final dinner and a gentle walk. Ginjinha (cherry liqueur) is a good last drink.

## Tips
- **Weather:** Late October is mild, around 18–22°C, with some chance of rain. Bring a light jacket.
- **Hills:** Lisbon is very hilly and the cobblestones are slippery. Wear comfortable shoes.
- **Getting around:** A Viva Viagem card works on the metro, trams and trains.
- **Pacing:** Plan only 1–2 main sights per day and leave gaps for café stops.
- **Check ahead:** Opening hours and tram or train schedules change, and Monday closures are common at some museums. Confirm before you go, and check the Gulbenkian's days.
