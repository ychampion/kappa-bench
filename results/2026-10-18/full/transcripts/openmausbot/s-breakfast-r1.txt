Three quick egg breakfasts:

1. **Scrambled egg toast.** Soft-scramble 2 eggs in butter, then pile them on buttered toast. Add chives or cheese if you have them. It takes about 5 minutes.
2. **Egg and veggie muffin cups.** Whisk eggs with spinach, tomato and cheese. Pour into a muffin tin and bake at 375°F for 15–18 minutes. Make a batch ahead and reheat through the week.
3. **Fried egg breakfast wrap.** Fry an egg and tuck it into a warm tortilla with avocado and salsa. It takes about 5 minutes and is easy to eat on the go.
